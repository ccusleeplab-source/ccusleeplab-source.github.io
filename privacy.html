Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

CCU Sleep Research Project ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data accessed through APIs is handled when you use [Your App Name].

1. Information We Access

When you authorize CCU Sleep Research Project via OAuth 2.0, the application accesses only the specific account and fitness data scopes you explicitly approve (such as basic profile, activity, steps, heart rate, and sleep information).

2. How We Use the Information

  • All retrieved data is used solely to sync and display your personal metrics into your own private storage (e.g., Google Sheets) for personal tracking.
  • We do not store your data on external public servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third parties or advertisers.

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Security & Token Storage

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely within your private script environment and are used exclusively to authenticate automated requests.

4. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You may revoke CCU Sleep Research Project's access at any time via your account permissions settings. Once revoked, the application will no longer be able to access your data.
