CCU Sleep Research Project

Welcome to CCU Sleep Research Project.

CCU Sleep Research Project is a personal data synchronization tool designed to connect with fitness APIs and export personal health metrics (such as daily steps, heart rate, and sleep logs) into personal Google Sheets for private tracking and analysis.

Key Features

  • Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Automated retrieval of personal activity and wellness data.
  • Read-only access strictly for personal record-keeping.
